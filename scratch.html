All About Soccer

What is Soccer?

Soccer, also known as football in most of the world, is a team sport played between two teams of 11 players. It is the most popular sport globally, known for its fast pace and passionate fans.

Basic Rules

  1. Each team tries to score by getting the ball into the opponent's goal.
  2. Players can't use their hands or arms — only the goalkeeper can within their own penalty area.
    • The goalkeeper can use their hands only within the penalty box.
    • They are the only player allowed to touch the ball with their hands.
  3. A standard match has two 45-minute halves.

Popular Teams

Some of the most famous soccer clubs in the world include FC Barcelona, Real Madrid, Manchester United, and Bayern Munich. National teams like Brazil, Germany, and Argentina are also iconic.

Why People Love Soccer

Soccer is a global sport that brings people together. It's simple to play, exciting to watch, and filled with drama, emotion, and unforgettable moments.
